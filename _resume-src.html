Autonomous Incident Desk - three-tier deflection ladder
  • Built a self-healing service desk that watches 15 production backends on a 15-second probe loop. Over 53 days it took 612 incidents, auto-resolved 72.9% of them at a 55-second mean time-to-resolution, and escalated 0.33% to a human.
